### Location map controls

Country and capital location games start in globe view. Drag to rotate and scroll
or pinch to zoom. Use **Flat map** to unfold the globe, and **Globe** to wrap it
back into a sphere. Your answers and highlighted countries stay the same.

In flat view, dragging and zooming stop at the map edges. Correct answers turn
the globe toward the country or move the flat map as close to its center as the
edges allow. Reduced-motion settings skip the transition animation. If WebGL is
unavailable, the game uses the original flat map.
